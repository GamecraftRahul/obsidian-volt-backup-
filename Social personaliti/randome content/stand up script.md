### Stand-Up Comedy Set

**[Walk on stage. Look at audience.]**

Last year hum ek trip pe gaye the…

**Bangalore.**

College ke classmates ke saath.

Aur college trip ka sabse bada advantage pata hai kya hai?

Aapko trip ka excitement nahi hota…

Aapko bas ye excitement hota hai ki—

**“Bhai… attendance ka kya hoga?”**

**[Pause]**

Trip pe jaane se pehle hum log bade excited the.

Aisa lag raha tha ki life mein pehli baar kuch adventurous karne wale hain.

Phir trip ke ek din pehle group mein message aaya—

**“Guys, subah 5 baje reporting hai.”**

Uske baad aadhe logon ka adventure khatam.

**[Pause]**

5 baje kaun uthta hai?

Hum college students hain…

Humare liye 5 baje **subah nahi hoti, raat ka extension hota hai.**

---

## HANUMAN JI WALA HILL

Bangalore ke aas-paas ek hill par hum gaye.

Wahan humein bataya gaya ki…

**“Yahin Hanuman ji ka janam hua tha.”**

Hum sab serious ho gaye.

Itni historical jagah hai…

To naturally hum log photo lene lage.

**[Look around]**

Indian tourist kisi bhi historical jagah pe jaake pehla kaam kya karta hai?

History feel nahi karta…

**Photo feel karta hai.**

“Bhai ek meri le.”

“Bhai landscape mein le.”

“Bhai ek aisi le jaise main yahan ka owner hoon.”

**[Pause]**

Aur ek dost toh itna serious pose de raha tha…

Jaise Hanuman ji ka janam nahi hua tha…

**uska khud ka janam hua tha wahan.**

---

Phir guide bata raha tha ki Hanuman ji bachpan mein Suraj ko fruit samajh ke khaane chale gaye the.

Main soch raha tha…

**Bhai eyesight toh bahut kharab thi.**

Suraj ko fruit kaise samajh liya?

Itna bada Suraj…

Aur itna chhota fruit?

**[Pause]**

Mujhe toh ye bhi lagta hai…

Hanuman ji ne Suraj ko fruit nahi samjha hoga.

Unhone bas socha hoga—

**“Itna bada hai… free mein mil raha hai… try karte hain.”**

**[Pause for laugh]**

Ye thought process hum Indians mein aaj bhi hai.

Shaadi mein khana free ho…

Toh hum bhi wahi karte hain.

**“Bhai plate bhar de… pata nahi dobara mile na mile.”**

---
## HILL CLIMBING

Phir hum log hill ke upar ja rahe the.

Shuru mein sab bahut energetic the.

**“Chal bhai! Chadhte hain!”**

Pehle 10 minute:

“Bhai kya view hai!”

20 minute baad:

“Bhai kitna aur hai?”

30 minute baad:

**“Bhai wapas neeche jaana hai kya?”**

**[Pause]**

Sabse dangerous sentence hota hai:

**“Bas thoda sa aur hai.”**

Ye “thoda sa” kabhi thoda nahi hota.

Indian parents bhi isi technique se bachchon ko manipulate karte hain.

“Bas 5 minute padh lo.”

Aur bachcha 2 ghante baad bhi book ke saamne baitha hota hai.

---
## AUTO WALA

Phir ek jagah jaane ke liye hum auto mein baithe.

Hum 5 log the.

Aur Indian auto wale ko agar 5 log dikh jaayein…

Toh uske dimaag mein meter nahi chalta.

**Calculator chalta hai.**

**[Pause]**

Hum:

“Bhaiya, itna paas hai.”

Auto wala:

“₹300.”

“Bhaiya ₹300 kyun?”

“Traffic hai.”

“Par bhaiya traffic toh hai hi nahi.”

“Road kharab hai.”

“Road bhi theek hai.”

“Petrol mehenga hai.”

**[Pause]**

Bhaiya…

Aap mujhe auto mein le ja rahe ho…

**Finance Ministry ka budget nahi bana rahe.**

---

Phir humne bargain kiya.

Hum students the.

Student card dikhaya.

Auto wale ne dekha…

Aur bola—

**“Student ho?”**

Hum:

“Haan.”

“Phir paise kahan se aa rahe hain?”

**[Pause]**

Sir…

**Isi wajah se toh bargain kar rahe hain!**

---
## ENDING / CALLBACK

Aur honestly…

Trip ka sabse achha part destination nahi tha.

Na Bangalore…

Na hill…

Na hotel…

Na auto.

Sabse achha part tha…

**hum 5 log ek saath survive kar gaye.**

Kyuki college mein classmates ko hum sirf class mein dekhte the.

Trip ke baad pata chala—

**Ye log mere classmates nahi hain…**

**Ye mere survival partners hain.**
